# A-1 — Welcome to B-GEN Picker

---

## Intro

Welcome to B-GEN Picker — a tool for Blender and Maya that lets you build a custom visual interface to declutter your workflow. Let me show you. Instead of your rig controls always obstructing your view and you strugling to select them, just set up your picker and hide all the controls. Now you can pick from a clearer interface and see your models move with zero visual clutter. 

To take it a step further, you have the flexibility to streamline your workflow on the Picker Canvas. You can add some custom buttons like the Pose button which lets you store poses and animation to reuse when ever you want. 

You can also add script buttons that give you the flexibility to  program it to do virtually what every you want. It could be to switch from IK to FK, Control the Level of Detail on your character or even Manage the assets in your scene.

B-GEN Picker works the same way in both Blender and Maya so this series applies to which ever platform you choose to use. I'll call out the few places where installation or 3d Software behavior differs.

In this series, we'll cover everything from installing the addon to shipping a full production-ready picker for a character. You'll learn how to create and style buttons, use every button mode — Select, Script, Pose, and Controller — organize your work across multiple tabs, share picker files and download from B-GEN Library.

## What you'll need

You'll need Blender versions from 3.5 upwards or Maya versions from 2022 upwards, a rig you want to build a picker for, and a few minutes per video. That's it.

## The plan

We'll start with the installation process, go over a quick tour of the interface, then move through button basics, each button mode, and finish with production workflows and B-GEN Library.

Let's get started.
