# F1 — Signing In to Your BGEN Account

**Target:** 2 min
**One-line hook:** Google OAuth / email OTP, active devices

---

## Intro

Everything we've covered so far works without an account. But **signing in** unlocks the BGEN **Marketplace**, saved payment methods, cross-device device management, and access to purchased pickers. Takes about a minute to set up.

## Where to sign in

Open **Settings → Account**. If you're not signed in, you'll see a prompt with two sign-in options: **Google** and **Email**.

## Option 1 — Google

Click **Sign in with Google**. BGEN opens your default browser and takes you through Google's standard sign-in flow. Approve the connection, and the browser hands control back to BGEN. You're in.

This is the fastest option, and it uses your Google profile picture as your BGEN avatar automatically.

## Option 2 — Email OTP

Prefer not to use Google? Click **Sign in with Email**. Type your email address, and BGEN sends a **6-digit one-time code** to your inbox. Paste the code into the dialog, and you're in.

No password — the OTP replaces it. Every time you sign in, you get a fresh code.

## The signed-in view

Once you're in, Settings → Account shows your profile:
- **Avatar** — Google profile pic, or your initials on a color background
- **Display name and email**
- **Active Devices** — every machine you're currently signed in on, with the last-active timestamp
- **Payment Methods** — saved credit cards (hidden by default, shown on click)
- **Sign Out** — just this device
- **Sign Out Everywhere** — every device at once

## Active devices

Every device that signs in to your account appears in the **Active Devices** list. If you sign in on a new laptop, you'll see it show up here next time you refresh. You can **sign out individual devices remotely** — useful if you install BGEN on a render node and want to kick it off later without physically touching that machine.

## A quick note

Your pickers are **local** — they don't upload to any server unless you're downloading from the marketplace or using cloud sync features. Signing in is about access to marketplace and account, not automatically uploading your work.
