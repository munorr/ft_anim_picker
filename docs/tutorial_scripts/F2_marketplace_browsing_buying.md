# F2 — Exploring the Marketplace — Browsing & Buying

**Target:** 3–4 min
**One-line hook:** Free vs paid, purchase flow, ratings, Marketplace library subfolder

---

## Intro

The **BGEN Marketplace** is where creators share and sell their pickers. Buy a picker for a rig you use, download it into your library, and you're set — no need to build from scratch. Let's walk through browsing, buying, and using a marketplace picker.

## Getting to the marketplace

Click the **Library** page icon, then switch to the **BGEN Library** sub-tab at the top. This is the marketplace.

## Browsing products

The marketplace shows a grid of **product cards**. Each card has:
- **Thumbnail** — a preview of the picker
- **Name** and creator
- **Software icon** — Blender or Maya (BGEN supports both)
- **Price** — in dollars, or green **FREE** for free items
- **Star rating** — average rating and number of reviews

You can sort by **featured**, **newest**, **most downloaded**, **highest rated**, or **price**. There's also a search bar and category filters.

## Product detail page

Click any product to see the full details — more thumbnails, a longer description, the creator's other products, and reviews from other users. If it's paid, the price and a **Buy** button are up top. If it's free, you just see a **Get** button.

## Buying a product

Click **Buy**. BGEN opens a **Stripe checkout page** in your browser. Enter your card details — or use a saved payment method if you have one — and confirm. Stripe processes the payment, and BGEN marks the product as owned in your account.

Saved payment methods live in **Settings → Account → Payment Methods**. Once a card is saved, future purchases are one-click.

## Downloading and using

After purchase, the product detail page switches to show a **Download** button. Click it, and BGEN pulls the `.bpicker` into your library — specifically into a **Marketplace** subfolder so marketplace-sourced pickers are easy to identify separately from your own.

Switch back to **My Library**, and the new picker is there ready to open. From that point, it behaves exactly like any picker you built yourself.

## Free items

Free items work the same way, just without a checkout step. Click **Get**, and the picker downloads into your Marketplace library subfolder.

## Rating a picker

Once you've owned and used a picker, the product detail page shows a **star rating input**. Click to give it 1 to 5 stars, optionally leave a comment, and your rating aggregates into the public average. This helps other buyers and rewards good creators.

## A quick note on refunds

BGEN supports refunds through Stripe's standard refund window. If a picker isn't what you expected, contact the creator or BGEN support — refund policies may vary by creator.
