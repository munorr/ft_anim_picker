# F3 — Following Creators & Managing Owned Items

**Target:** 2–3 min
**One-line hook:** Creator profiles, follow, re-download from purchase history

---

## Intro

Once you've bought a few pickers, you'll want to stay up to date with the creators who make them, and have a way to re-download stuff you've already paid for. This video covers **creator profiles**, **following**, and the **Owned Items** page.

## Creator profiles

Every picker in the marketplace has a **creator** attached to it. Click the creator's name or avatar on a product card to open their **profile page**.

A creator profile shows:
- **Avatar and display name**
- **Bio** — who they are, what they make
- **Follower count**
- **All their products** — usually rig-specific pickers, but some creators also publish pose libraries, face rigs, or general-purpose templates

## Following a creator

On a creator's profile, click **Follow**. They get added to a short list you can access from Settings. When a creator you follow publishes a new product, BGEN can notify you — great for staying on top of updates for rigs you actively use.

Unfollow by clicking again. Your follow list is private — creators can see their follower **count** but not **who** follows them.

## Owned Items

From **Settings → Account**, click the **Owned Items** button. This opens a page listing every product you've purchased or claimed.

Each owned item has:
- **Thumbnail and name**
- **Purchase date** and price paid
- **Download** button — re-download the `.bpicker` if you deleted it from your library, changed machines, or switched to a new install

**Re-downloading costs nothing**. Once you've bought a product, it's yours to download as many times as you need.

## Managing your library from Owned Items

If you want to **open** a product directly, the Owned Items page has a quick link that takes you to that `.bpicker` in your library. Useful when you're trying to remember if you've already downloaded something.

## A creator workflow tip

When you find a rig creator whose pickers fit your workflow — clean naming, good coverage, clear visual organization — follow them and check their profile periodically. Creators often release **updates** to existing pickers (new features, fixes for rig updates), and your already-purchased item just auto-updates the file you'd re-download.
